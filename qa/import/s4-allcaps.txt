COCHLEAR IMPLANT SURGERY: WHAT TO DO AT HOME

DRESSING
Your head dressing comes off two days after surgery. You do not need to replace it.

INCISION CARE
Do not scrub the incision. Apply a thin layer of antibiotic ointment twice daily once the tape falls off.

BATHING
You may shower after 3 days. Keep the incision dry for 1 week.

MEDICATIONS
You have been given a prescription for an antibiotic. Take all of it.
Tylenol or the prescription pain medicine as needed. No aspirin or ibuprofen for 2 weeks.

ACTIVITY
No lifting, bending or straining for 2 weeks. No air travel for 1 week.

WHEN TO CALL US
Fever above 101.5 F, drainage, spreading redness, or a headache that will not go away.

APPOINTMENTS
Wound check: 10/03/2026
Activation: 4 weeks after surgery
