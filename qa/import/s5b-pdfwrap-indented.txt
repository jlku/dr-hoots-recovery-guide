Cochlear Implant Post-Operative Care Instructions

Dressing: You will go home with a large dressing wrapped around your head. This dressing should
   remain in place for two full days. On the third morning you may remove it and discard it.

Incision: There are steri-strips or surgical tape over your incision. Do not pull them off. They
   will fall off by themselves in about ten days. Do not put any cream, ointment, peroxide or
   alcohol on the incision unless we tell you to.

Bathing: You may shower 72 hours after surgery. Do not allow water to run directly over the
   incision, and do not wash your hair in a tub or sink where the ear could be submerged. No
   swimming for four weeks.

Medications: Take the antibiotic that was prescribed to you until it is gone. For pain, use
   acetaminophen. Do not take ibuprofen, naproxen, aspirin or any other non-steroidal anti-
   inflammatory medication for ten days after surgery because of bleeding risk.

Follow up: Your wound check appointment is in two to three weeks. Your implant will be activated
   approximately one month after surgery.
