Post-Op Instructions - Cochlear Implant

Discharge Medications
Medication          Dose        Route   Frequency       Duration        Notes
Cephalexin          500 mg      PO      q8h             7 days          Take all doses
Acetaminophen       650 mg      PO      q6h PRN         as needed       Max 3000 mg/day
Oxycodone           5 mg        PO      q6h PRN         2 days          Severe pain only
Ondansetron         4 mg        ODT     q8h PRN         3 days          Nausea
Ciprodex otic       4 drops     Otic    BID             7 days          Operative ear only

Do not take: ibuprofen, naproxen, aspirin, Aleve, Motrin, Advil for 10 days.

Wound care: head dressing off in 2 days, steri-strips stay until they fall off, shower at 72 hours.
Activity: no lifting over 10 pounds for 2 weeks, no nose blowing, no swimming for 4 weeks.
Follow-up appointment: 2 weeks post-op for wound check.
Device activation: 4-6 weeks post-op with audiology.
Questions: call 206-555-0117 during business hours or the answering service after hours.
