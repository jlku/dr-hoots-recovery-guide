COCHLEAR IMPLANT - POST OPERATIVE INSTRUCTIONS

1. Leave the mastoid pressure dressing in place for 48 hours. After that you may remove it yourself.
2. Do not remove the steri-strips over the incision. They will loosen and fall off in 7-10 days.
3. No shampooing or showering for 72 hours. After 72 hours you may shower; keep the incision out of the direct stream.
4. No swimming, no hot tubs, and no submerging your head for 3 weeks.
5. Take your antibiotic (cephalexin 500 mg three times daily) until it is finished.
6. Pain: oxycodone-acetaminophen 5/325 one tablet every 6 hours as needed for severe pain for the first 2 days, then switch to plain acetaminophen.
7. Do NOT take ibuprofen, Motrin, Advil, Aleve, naproxen or aspirin for 10 days.
8. No heavy lifting over 10 lbs and no strenuous activity for 2 weeks.
9. Do not blow your nose. Sneeze with your mouth open.
10. Call our office at 312-555-0188 for fever over 101.5 F, worsening pain, redness, swelling, or drainage.
11. Return to clinic for a wound check in 2 weeks.
12. Activation with audiology is scheduled approximately 4 weeks after surgery.
