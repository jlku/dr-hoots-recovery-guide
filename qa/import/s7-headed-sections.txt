POST-OPERATIVE CARE AFTER EAR SURGERY

Activity:
  • Rest at home for the first two days. No lifting over 10 pounds for two weeks.
  • No swimming or diving until your surgeon says it is safe.

Care of the incision:
  • Leave the head dressing in place until the day after surgery, then remove it at home.
  • Apply a thin layer of antibiotic ointment to the incision twice a day until it is dry.

Pain:
  • Take acetaminophen as directed on the bottle for discomfort.
  • Avoid aspirin, ibuprofen and other anti-inflammatory medicines for ten days.

Follow up:
  • You will have important follow-up appointments.
  • First visit in 1 week, then again at 4 weeks. Activation is usually 2-4 weeks after surgery.

When to call us:
  • Fever over 101.5 F, drainage from the wound, or spreading redness behind the ear.
  • Dizziness that does not settle, or any weakness of the face.
