03 · Visual grammar

Safety-card primitives

These are the reusable marks around an illustration—not illustrations themselves. The finished guide combines people, objects, actions, close-ups, and warnings in one continuous sequence.

04 · Applied card

One continuous recovery card

Every canonical scene becomes an illustrated frame in one vertical document. Scrolling reveals the next frame; it never hides, rewrites, or times the instruction.

The current content maps to 14 frames in three sections. The card is authored first as a complete static document. Motion is added afterward as progressive enhancement.
