# Dr. Hoots generation prompts

These are the exact prompts used for the version-one character assets. The built-in generator did not expose a seed; source hashes and rendered output hashes are recorded in `assets/character/manifest.json`.

## turnaround-v1

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production character turnaround sheet for an accessible patient-education mascot
Primary request: Create one clean character turnaround sheet of the exact Dr. Hoots clay owl shown in the reference, with four full-body views: front, three-quarter, left profile, and back. This is identity documentation, not a new redesign.
Input images: Image 1 is the identity reference and must be preserved.
Scene/backdrop: flat pale-blue studio background, no room or props.
Subject: the same adult owl clinician with identical round head, cream eye disks, very large ivory eyes with black pupils, orange beak, brown brow feathers and ear tufts, compact body, teal V-neck scrub top, two lower patch pockets, one small chest pocket, and one plain round gold pin.
Style/medium: tactile stop-motion clay model, finely textured feathers and fabric, consistent materials in every view.
Composition/framing: four evenly spaced full-body views at the same scale, feet visible, orthographic character-sheet presentation, generous margins.
Lighting/mood: soft neutral studio light, calm and trustworthy.
Color palette: preserve the exact brown, cream, orange, teal, and gold palette from the reference.
Constraints: all four views must depict one identical model; preserve facial proportions, eye size and spacing, beak shape, brow feathers, body proportions, scrub construction, pockets, pin, limbs, texture, and scale. Neutral closed-mouth expression. Wings relaxed at sides. No labels or text.
Avoid: redesign, youthfulness, comedy, smile changes, extra limbs or fingers, malformed wings, drifting clothing, missing or duplicated pockets, mutated pin, letters, logos, medical symbols, stethoscope, clipboard, props, shadows that obscure silhouette, cropped feet, watermark.
```
